Open D tuning

Open D tuning is D A D F♯ A D: lower the 6th, 2nd and 1st strings a whole step and the 3rd string a half step. The open strings ring a D major chord.

Open D keeps the root on both the lowest and highest strings, so open chords sound full and wide.

Tap a string above to hear its note, or turn on the mic: the string you're tuning lights up and glows brighter as you get close.

How to tune to Open D

  1. Lower the 6th string from E to D.
  2. Lower the 3rd string from G to F♯ (a half step).
  3. Lower the 2nd string from B to A.
  4. Lower the 1st string from E to D.

Questions

What chord do the open strings make in open D?

D major: the notes are D, F♯ and A.
