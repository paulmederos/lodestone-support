D standard tuning

D standard tuning is D G C F A D: every string lowered a whole step from standard.

How to tune to D standard

  1. Lower every string by a whole step (200 cents): E to D, A to G, D to C, G to F, B to A, E to D.

Questions

How is D standard different from drop D?

Drop D lowers only the 6th string (D A D G B E). D standard lowers all six strings a whole step (D G C F A D).
