DADGAD tuning

DADGAD tuning is D A D G A D, from the thickest string to the thinnest. From standard, lower the 6th, 2nd and 1st strings a whole step.

Frequencies use A4 = 440 Hz. Strings are numbered from the thinnest.

The open strings make a chord that's neither major nor minor, which is why DADGAD is loved for Celtic and folk fingerstyle playing.

Tap a string above to hear its note, or turn on the mic: the string you're tuning lights up and glows brighter as you get close.

How to tune to DADGAD

  1. Lower the 6th string from E to D.
  2. Lower the 2nd string from B to A.
  3. Lower the 1st string from E to D. The A, D and G strings stay put.

Questions

Is DADGAD major or minor?

Neither. The open strings are D, A and G, with no third, so they sound open and ringing (a Dsus4 chord).
