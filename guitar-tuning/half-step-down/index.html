Half step down tuning

Half step down tuning is E♭ A♭ D♭ G♭ B♭ E♭, from the thickest string to the thinnest: every string lowered one half step from standard. It's also called E♭ standard.

The chord shapes don't change, so everything you know in standard tuning still works. It all just sounds a half step lower, and the strings feel a little looser.

How to tune to Half step down

  1. Lower every string by one half step (100 cents): E to E♭, A to A♭, D to D♭, G to G♭, B to B♭, E to E♭.

Questions

Is half step down the same as E flat tuning?

Yes. Half step down, E♭ standard and E♭ tuning all mean E♭ A♭ D♭ G♭ B♭ E♭.
